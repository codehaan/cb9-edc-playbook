CB9 is a volunteer, advisory Community Board. We don’t issue permits or licenses, inspect businesses, or set or cancel fines. This site explains official resources in plain language and links to them. Always confirm details with the agency.

Help from CB9 is always free. Be careful with anyone who charges you to fill out a free City or State form.

In an emergency, call 911. For City services and questions, call 311.
